O MoStay-u About MoStay

Inspirisan mediteranskim
duhom Mostara Inspired by the
Mediterranean
soul of Mostar

Inspirisan mediteranskim karakterom Mostara, MoStay Boutique Hotel nudi 26 pažljivo dizajniranih soba inspirisanih prirodnim teksturama, toplim tonovima i opuštenim načinom života južne Bosne i Hercegovine. Bilo da provodite dan lutajući starim kamenim ulicama, otkrivajući skrivene kafiće, ili jednostavno uživajući u mirnoj večeri u hotelu — svaki trenutak u MoStay-u je dizajniran da se osjeća opušteno, elegantno i nezaboravno. Inspired by the Mediterranean character of Mostar, MoStay Boutique Hotel offers 26 carefully designed rooms inspired by natural textures, warm tones, and the relaxed lifestyle of southern Bosnia and Herzegovina. Whether you spend your day wandering the old stone streets, discovering hidden cafés, or simply enjoying a quiet evening at the hotel — every moment at MoStay is designed to feel effortless, elegant, and memorable.
