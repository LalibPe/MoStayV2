About MoStay

Inspired by the
Mediterranean
soul of Mostar

Inspired by the Mediterranean character of Mostar, MoStay Boutique Hotel offers 26 carefully designed rooms inspired by natural textures, warm tones, and the relaxed lifestyle of southern Bosnia and Herzegovina. Whether you spend your day wandering the old stone streets, discovering hidden cafés, or simply enjoying a quiet evening at the hotel — every moment at MoStay is designed to feel effortless, elegant, and memorable.
